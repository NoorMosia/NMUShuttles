Project Overview

Provide a 75–100 word overview of your project.

Briefly explain:
• the problem your project addresses;
• who the intended users are;
• what your system does; and
• what makes your solution useful or interesting.

Write for a general/industry audience rather than as a technical report.

---

## Answer

NMU students queue at shuttle stops before dawn with no guaranteed seat. With no way to know in advance, students either leave unsafely early or get stranded with no time to arrange another way to class. Our Android app digitizes the stop-queue-scan workflow students, drivers, and marshals already know, then improves it with real-time booking, QR boarding passes, and a nightly job that pre-clears accessibility-cleared students onto accessible shuttles before the general rush opens. A separate job locks trips an hour out and auto-promotes waitlisted students on cancellation, turning a manual queue into a fair, data-driven, safer system.
