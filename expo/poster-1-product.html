Key Features
event_seat

Seat Booking

Students book a seat on scheduled trips up to 3 days ahead and receive a QR boarding pass. A waitlist fills automatically when a trip is full.

accessible

Priority Accessibility

Disability-cleared students get automatic recurring pre-bookings, assigned to the front accessible shuttles in the convoy.

qr_code_scanner

Marshal Check-In

Field marshals scan passenger QR codes to verify boarding. Scanning opens 35 minutes before departure.

dashboard

Admin Portal

Full control over stops, shuttles, users, schedules and calendar exceptions — plus vendor reliability analytics and report handling.

bolt

Automated Operations

A nightly job creates trips 3 days ahead and injects priority bookings. A lockout gate locks trips at T-60 and promotes the waitlist.

build

Breakdown Handling

A marshal flags a broken shuttle, an admin resolves it (replace or cancel), and affected riders get a push notification only after resolution.

How a Trip Works
1

Book

Student reserves a seat and gets a QR pass, up to 3 days ahead.

2

Lock

At T-60 min the trip locks and the waitlist is promoted FIFO.

3

Scan

From T-35 min the marshal scans passengers at the boarding point.

4

Board

Riders fill the front shuttle first across the convoy.
